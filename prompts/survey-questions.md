# Classmate survey (5 minutes; use answers in the submission)
1. Where do you check your schedule now? What annoys you about it?
2. Which dean's office task wastes the most time (certificates, applications, grades)?
3. How often do you miss an announcement or deadline? Where do you see announcements?
4. Do you mostly use phone or laptop for study matters?
5. What 3 features would make you open this every day?
6. Would you trust an AI assistant for "when is my next exam?" (yes/no, why)
7. For international students: what is hardest to understand (language, procedures, documents)?
